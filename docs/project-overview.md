# Project overview

Sarathi is a Family Health Agent prototype: your family care companion, across generations. It helps families organize health records and manage view-only sharing.

The app includes fixed-OTP signup/login, Supabase metadata, Google Drive OAuth/storage, document upload and previews, member grants and revocation, email outbox and Gmail notifications, Setu eSign consent gating, and optional Groq responses. Appointment booking remains a future workflow. See `healthnest-repository.md` for provider setup and prototype limitations.
